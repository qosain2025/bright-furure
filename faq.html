Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about admissions, fees, course curriculum, or lab equipment? Find direct and comprehensive answers below.

Where is Bright Future Academy (BFA) located?
BFA is located on Main New City Road, near Read Foundation School, New City, Chakswari, Azad Kashmir. It is easily accessible by public and private transport with ample parking nearby.
Which courses and diplomas are offered?
We offer the 6-Month Professional IT Diploma, 3-Month Fast Track Computer Course, IELTS Academic & General Preparation, Spoken English & Communication Skills, AI Tools Mastery, Canva Graphic Design, Digital Marketing, and UK A1/Life in the UK visa test preparation.
Are the computer classes 100% practical in the lab?
Yes, absolutely. We strictly maintain a hands-on learning environment. Every enrolled student has their own assigned computer workstation with modern hardware, high-speed internet, and all necessary software tools.
How long does the IELTS preparation course take?
Standard IELTS preparation usually spans 2 to 3 months depending on your baseline English proficiency. We also offer fast-track 4-week crash courses for candidates who already have basic proficiency and only require test techniques and mock assessments.
What are the class batch timings?
We offer multiple shifts to suit your lifestyle: Morning (09:00 AM – 12:00 PM), Afternoon (02:00 PM – 04:30 PM), and Evening (04:30 PM – 07:00 PM). Saturdays are designated for comprehensive mock exams and special speaking clubs.
Can I get fee confirmation and discounts before admission?
Yes, our fee schedules are completely transparent (PKR 3,000/month for Computer Course, PKR 3,500/month for Spoken English, and packages from PKR 15,000 for IELTS). Special package discounts are available if you enroll in multiple modules or pay upfront.
Do you issue completion certificates?
Yes! Upon successfully finishing your course and completing all practical lab assignments and final examinations, students receive official Bright Future Academy certificates that substantiate their skills on job applications and CVs.
Can I contact BFA on WhatsApp for instant answers?
Yes, our WhatsApp hotline (03448343097) is active daily from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Click the WhatsApp button anywhere on this website to speak directly with an admissions advisor.
